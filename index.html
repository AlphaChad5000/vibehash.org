For computers with time to spare

Join as a miner

Your GPU, or your processor if you don't have one, trains pieces of the network's jobs and checks other miners' pieces. Work that checks out is paid in test VIBE.

    Install

    Paste the install line above, or download the app. It needs Python 3.10 or newer; the Windows installer adds it.

  3. 3
    Mine

02 · How it works

One round, about every 40 seconds

Each block seals one round. A round has four phases, the same four the worker's dashboard shows. Jobs with budget left take turns, one round each.

  1. REGISTER

    Sign up for the round

    Workers register for the round, and the node hands each a few units: training steps of the job whose turn it is.

  2. COMPUTE

    Train and commit

    Each worker trains its units on its GPU or CPU, uploads the checkpoints, and commits to its results before anyone can see them.

  3. REVEAL

    Show the work

    Workers reveal what they committed to, and the round's random beacon picks which pieces get checked.

  4. VERIFY

    Check, then pay

    Other workers redo the chosen pieces, which must match to within 1%. A disagreement narrows to one calculation the network redoes, and whoever was wrong is slashed.

03 · Research

Evo Hash: training a language model by evolution, on the chain

Evo Hash trains with evolution strategies instead of gradients: each round, miners score twin pairs of small random changes to the model, and the chain keeps only the seeds and the scores, a few hundred bytes a generation. Anyone can rebuild the model from the chain. Base model: Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct. Every score below is 200 held-out questions per set.

06 · Questions

Questions people ask

Is VIBE worth money?+

No. This is a test network and test VIBE has no cash value, so don't buy or sell it. It's how the network keeps score of who did the work.

What does it do to my computer?+

While you mine it uses your graphics card or processor for training. It asks before any big download, keeps model files only where and as long as you allow, and stops with Ctrl+C.

How is cheating stopped?+

Workers commit to their results before revealing them, and other workers redo randomly chosen pieces. A disagreement narrows to one calculation the network redoes, and whoever was wrong loses stake, so faking work or falsely accusing someone costs more than it gains.

Who runs the network?+

Today a single validator, operated by the project's founder. The software already supports many validators voting on every block; adding more is the next step.

Is this connected to DeepSeek or Qwen?+

No. Vibe Hash isn't affiliated with or endorsed by them. Their open models are among the models the network fine-tunes, under their licences.
